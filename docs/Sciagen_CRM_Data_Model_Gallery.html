Sciagen CRM / Account Management

Account Management Data Model

Entities and relationships for healthcare professionals (HCPs) and organisations (HCOs), their affiliations, influence networks and professional activity.

Scroll or zoom the diagram. In the PDF it is scaled to the page.

Entities/Objects Included

Affiliation, Best Office Time, Clinical Trial, Congress Event, Influence Peer, Publication, Sciagen Account

How it works in our org

  • One object, Sciagen Account, holds both HCPs and HCOs; Type decides which. Accounts can have a parent account and a merge pointer to the surviving record.
  • Affiliation links an HCP to an HCO. Influence Peer links an HCP to the peers who influence them.
  • Best Office Time, Publications, Clinical Trials and Congress Events hang off the HCP or HCO and feed the account profile on the iPad and in the account page.
  • Rows reach a rep through territory sharing (see Territory Management); the object is private by default.

Sciagen CRM / Duplicate Management

Duplicate Management Data Model

Entities and relationships for detecting, reviewing and merging duplicate accounts, with a log that allows a merge to be reverted.

Scroll or zoom the diagram. In the PDF it is scaled to the page.

Entities/Objects Included

Duplicate Candidate, Duplicate Setting, Merge Log, Sciagen Account

How it works in our org

  • A candidate pairs two accounts (Record A and Record B) with a suggested winner and a review status.
  • A merge writes a Merge Log with the winner, loser and who ran it, so it can be reverted.
  • The losing account stays in the system, marked merged and pointing at the winner. Triggers on call reports, plans, consent and similar objects redirect any stale reference from an iPad to the winner.

Sciagen CRM / Territory Management

Territory Management Data Model

Entities and relationships for the territory hierarchy, rep-to-territory and account-to-territory alignment, and product allocation by territory.

Scroll or zoom the diagram. In the PDF it is scaled to the page.

Entities/Objects Included

Account Territory Assignment, Product, Product Territory Assignment, Sciagen Account, Territory, User Territory Assignment

How it works in our org

  • Territories form a hierarchy through Parent Territory.
  • User Territory assigns a rep to a territory; its name defaults to the rep's name.
  • Account Territory aligns an account to a territory. Triggers use these two links to share accounts, segmentation, data change requests, RCPA audits and planned calls with every rep in the territory.
  • Product Territory Assignment states which products are sold in which territory.

How it works in our org

How it works in our org

  • A Presentation is a deck of Slides for a product and moves through Draft, Medical, Regulatory and Legal and Compliance approval to Approved Promotional, then Expired. Only approved decks are shown to reps.

  • Key Messages are captured per call report and session. Favourites and My Presentations are per-rep shortcuts.

Sample Limits and Compliance Data Model

Entities and relationships for per-product sample limits, overrides, blocked attempts, expenditure tracking and the UCPMP compliance configuration.

Scroll or zoom the diagram. In the PDF it is scaled to the page.

Entities/Objects Included

Product, Sample Attempt, Sample Blocked Attempt, Sample Drop, Sample Expenditure Tracking, Sample Limit, Sample Limit Override, Sciagen Account, UCPMP Tenant Config

How it works in our org

  • A Sample Limit caps packs per call, day, week, month, quarter or financial year, scoped to HCP and product, HCP overall, rep and product, rep overall or territory and product. It can be regulatory (UCPMP) or company, hard-block or warning.
  • A Sample Limit Override (master-detail child of the limit) is a manager-approved exception: increase, exempt or one-time waiver, for a doctor or rep, with validity dates.
  • When a drop would break a limit, the iPad publishes a Sample Attempt event and Salesforce records a Sample Blocked Attempt.
  • Sample Expenditure Tracking keeps the running total given to one doctor for one product in a financial year, the basis of the UCPMP disclosure. UCPMP Tenant Config holds company details and compliance switches.
