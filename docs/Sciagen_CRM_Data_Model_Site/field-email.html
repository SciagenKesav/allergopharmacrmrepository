Sciagen CRM / Field Email

Field Email Data Model

Entities and relationships for approved email templates, the content allowed in them, and emails sent to HCPs.

Scroll or zoom the diagram. In the PDF it is scaled to the page.

Entities/Objects Included

Approved Content, Approved Email Template, Product, Sciagen Account, Sent Email, Sent Email Attachment, Template Allowed Content

How it works in our org

  • Approved Email Templates are tied to a product and list which Approved Content may be attached.
  • Sent Email records what was sent to which HCP, from which template, with its attachments.
