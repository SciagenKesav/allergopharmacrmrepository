Sciagen CRM / Expenses and Leave

Expenses and Leave Data Model

Entities and relationships for daily expense claims, travel patches, monthly allowance claims and leave requests.

Scroll or zoom the diagram. In the PDF it is scaled to the page.

Entities/Objects Included

Daily Expense Bill, Daily Expense Claim, Expense Rate Card, Expense Travel Patch, Leave Entitlement, Leave Request, Monthly Allowance Claim, Monthly Claim Bill, User

How it works in our org

  • A Daily Expense Claim has bills and travel patches. A Monthly Allowance Claim has bills for the month.
  • Rates and leave entitlements come from custom metadata. A Leave Request goes to a manager for approval.
