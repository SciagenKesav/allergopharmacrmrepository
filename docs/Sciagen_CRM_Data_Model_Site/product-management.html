Sciagen CRM / Product Management

Product Management Data Model

Entities and relationships for the product catalogue, product hierarchy, divisions, molecules, targets and secondary sales.

Scroll or zoom the diagram. In the PDF it is scaled to the page.

Entities/Objects Included

Division, Molecule, Product, Product Master, Sales Target, Sales Transaction, Sciagen Account, Territory

How it works in our org

  • Product is the working catalogue with a parent product and a parent detail product.
  • Product Master holds the master data; Division and Molecule are custom metadata used by pickers.
  • Sales Target sets a target per product and territory; Sales Transaction records secondary sales through a stockist.
