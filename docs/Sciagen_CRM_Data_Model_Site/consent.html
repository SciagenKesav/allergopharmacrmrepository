Consent Management Data Model

Entities and relationships for HCP consent, with channel, email and product preferences.

Scroll or zoom the diagram. In the PDF it is scaled to the page.

Entities/Objects Included

Consent — Channel Preference, Consent — Email Channel, Consent — Product Preference, HCP Consent, Product, Sciagen Account

How it works in our org

  • An HCP Consent record holds who the HCP is, the purposes consented (Email, Mobile, WhatsApp, Sample Opt-In), verification flags, and the signature with its meaning and date.
  • Child records store the preferred channels, the email address consented for, and the products consented for. Sample Opt-In has a validity date used by the sample rules.
