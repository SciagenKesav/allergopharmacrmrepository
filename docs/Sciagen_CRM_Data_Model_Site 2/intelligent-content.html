Sciagen CRM / Intelligent Content

Intelligent Content Data Model

Entities and relationships for approved presentations, slides, CLM sessions, key messages and slide-level engagement tracking.

Scroll or zoom the diagram. In the PDF it is scaled to the page.

Entities/Objects Included

Approved Content, Call Visit Report, Content Alert, Key Message, My Presentation, Presentation, Presentation Favorite, Presentation Session, Product, Slide, Slide Event

How it works in our org

  • A Presentation is a deck of Slides for a product and moves through Draft, Medical, Regulatory and Legal and Compliance approval to Approved Promotional, then Expired. Only approved decks are shown to reps.
  • When a rep presents on the iPad, a Presentation Session is recorded against the call report, with a Slide Event for each slide shown and the time spent.
  • Key Messages are captured per call report and session. Favourites and My Presentations are per-rep shortcuts.
  • A session sets the report's Call Category to Call with CLM. Content Alerts (New, Updated, Expiring) tell reps when a deck changes.
