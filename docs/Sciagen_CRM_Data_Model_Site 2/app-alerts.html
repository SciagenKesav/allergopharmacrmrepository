Entities/Objects Included

Content Alert, Presentation, Sciagen Alert

How it works in our org

  • Sciagen Alert is a message with a title, type (Company, Content or DCR), priority, audience (All users, a profile or specific users) and active dates.
  • Content Alert flags a presentation as New, Updated or Expiring.
