Sciagen CRM / Sample Management

Sample Management Data Model

Entities and relationships for sample stock, warehouses, lots, allocations, transfers and drops to HCPs.

Scroll or zoom the diagram. In the PDF it is scaled to the page.

Entities/Objects Included

Call Visit Report, Product, Sample Allocation, Sample Drop, Sample Lot, Sample Transaction, Sample Warehouse, Sciagen Account

How it works in our org

  • Stock lives in Sample Lots (batch, expiry, cold-chain data), each held in a Sample Warehouse or by a rep, per product.
  • Every movement is a Sample Transaction: Receipt, Disbursement, Transfer Out, Transfer In, Return or Adjustment. Transfers are paired through Paired Transaction, and a transaction can need approval.
  • A Sample Drop ties a disbursement to a call report and an HCP, and carries the HCP signature (in person or remote link / QR).
  • Sample Allocation gives a rep or territory a quantity of a product for a period and tracks used, remaining and utilisation.
