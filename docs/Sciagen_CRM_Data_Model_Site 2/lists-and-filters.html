Lists and Filters Data Model

Entities and relationships for custom HCP lists, saved views and rep preferences.

Scroll or zoom the diagram. In the PDF it is scaled to the page.

Entities/Objects Included

Custom List, Custom List Member, Rep Preference, Saved View, Sciagen Account

How it works in our org

  • A Custom List groups HCPs; members are rows in Custom List Member.
  • Saved Filters keep a rep's favourite views of the schedule and account browser; Rep Preference stores per-rep settings.
