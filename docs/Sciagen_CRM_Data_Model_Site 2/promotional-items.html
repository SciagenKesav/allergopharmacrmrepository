Sciagen CRM / Promotional Items

Promotional Items Data Model

Entities and relationships for promotional items (notepads, pens and similar) and the drops made on a visit.

Scroll or zoom the diagram. In the PDF it is scaled to the page.

Entities/Objects Included

Call Visit Report, Product, Promotional Item, Promotional Item Drop

How it works in our org

  • Promotional Item is the catalogue of items, optionally tied to a product.
  • A Promo Drop records which item was given on which call report and how many.
