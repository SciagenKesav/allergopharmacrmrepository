Key Account Management - Account Plan Data Model

Entities and relationships for account plans with goals, stakeholders, strategies, activities, action items and the account team.

Scroll or zoom the diagram. In the PDF it is scaled to the page.

Entities/Objects Included

Account Plan, Account Team Member, Action Item, Key Stakeholder, Plan Activity, Plan Goal, Plan Strategy, Product Plan Template, Product Strategy, Sciagen Account

How it works in our org

  • An Account Plan belongs to an HCP or an HCO. Goals, stakeholders, strategies and the account team are its children.
  • Strategies contain activities, and activities contain action items; an action item can be completed on a call report.
  • Product Plan Template and Product Strategy supply pre-built strategies per product.
  • Key Account Managers can edit any plan (a sharing rule); call reports and planned calls can point at a plan.
