Sciagen CRM / Visit Management

Visit Management Data Model

Entities and relationships for planning, executing and reporting on visits to healthcare professionals and organisations, including group calls and signature capture.

Scroll or zoom the diagram. In the PDF it is scaled to the page.

Entities/Objects Included

Account Plan, CVR Attendee, CVR Internal Attendee, Call Detail Priority, Call Visit Report, Planned Call / Event, Sciagen Account, Support Request, Territory

How it works in our org

  • A rep schedules a Planned Call (visit or event). When the visit is done, a Call Visit Report is created and linked both ways: the planned call's Resulting CVR and the report's Source Planned Call.
  • Call visits can also be reported without a plan (walk-in). A report moves through Draft, Submitted and Saved; once Submitted it is locked, and only an approved Unlock Request (a support request) reopens it.
  • A group call is one parent report plus one report per attending HCP, linked through Parent Call. Internal colleagues are recorded in CVR Internal Attendee.
  • Call Detail Priority records the products detailed, in order, with slides shown and time spent. Call Category carries the label the iPad shows (Call Only or Call with CLM).
  • Check-in location, accuracy and geofence result are stored on the report; signature fields capture the HCP's sign-off.
