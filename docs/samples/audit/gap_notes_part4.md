## 1. High-risk area #4 — UCPMP compliance rules (SM-UC-01..16)

The rules engine that exists is real and better than a stub in several places, but it is uneven, and the unevenness tracks exactly the clauses that are easiest to code versus the ones that require a richer data model. The **12-packs-per-drug-per-HCP-per-year rule (SM-UC-05)** is the strongest result in this whole slice: `SampleLimitSeeder.cls` seeds a hard-block, non-deletable, non-relaxable `Sample_Limit__c` record (`Limit_Quantity__c = 12`, `Is_Regulatory__c = true`, `Hard_Block__c = true`), `SampleLimitChecker.cls` counts usage from **completed disbursements by HCP+Product regardless of which rep performed them** (i.e. genuinely company-wide, all-channel, as the clause requires), and `SampleLimitGuardService.cls:10-23` blocks any attempt to delete, relax, disable, or raise it. That is exactly the shape the spec asks for on the server. The **3-patient pack rule (SM-UC-04)** and **smallest-market-pack rule (SM-UC-07)** are the opposite story: the fields exist (`Max_Patients_Per_Pack__c`, `Smallest_Market_Pack_Size__c`) but there is no `Course_Of_Treatment_Units__c` field and no validation rule anywhere cross-checking `Pack_Size__c` against either — `Product__c` has zero entries under `validationRules/`, confirmed by directory listing. The **"not for sale" legend (SM-UC-06)** is half-real: `Sample_Transaction__c.Marked_Not_For_Sale__c` defaults true and the disbursement signing statement (`SampleDropService.cls:148`) literally tells the HCP the pack is marked "free medical sample not for sale," but nothing gates lot *activation* on a legend attestation the way the spec wants. The **hypnotic/sedative/tranquillizer block (SM-UC-09)** works mechanically — `Is_Restricted__c` + `Restriction_Reason__c = 'Hypnotic_Sedative_Tranquillizer'` is hard-blocked in both `SampleEligibilityService.cls:54-57` and `SampleTransactionService.cls:143` — but it rides on the same generic "restricted" checkbox used for supply shortages and regulatory holds; there is no dedicated, mandatory therapeutic classification that would make a hypnotic product self-enforcing rather than admin-discretionary. If an admin leaves `Is_Restricted__c` unticked on a genuinely hypnotic product, nothing in this system stops it being sampled — that is flagged as a CONFLICT risk against the spec's "can never be sampled" language, not just a missing field. The **2% cap (SM-UC-11)** and **5.1(g) per-unit valuation (SM-UC-12)** are both partially built: a working dashboard (`UCPMPComplianceService.getExpenditureSummary()`) computes percent-of-sales and percent-of-cap with a status label, and `Sample_Unit_Value_INR__c` is genuinely snapshotted onto every transaction at time of supply — but the valuation model is a single current value per product with no FY-scoped price master, no separate price-to-stockist vs purchase-price distinction, and no provisional-vs-final variance at FY close, which clause 5.1(g) and Circular 3/2025 both specifically require. The **₹1,000 informational-item cap (SM-UC-13)** and the **authorized-receiver model (part of SM-UC-02)** are both simply absent — no object, no field, no check. On the **iPad side, every one of these 16 rules is either MISSING or actively CONFLICTING** with the server rule it should mirror: the only "sample limit" logic on device is a hardcoded, non-UCPMP, display-only cap on exactly 3 named products (Emrok 48/yr, Glaritus 36/yr, Miqnaf 60/yr — not the legal 12), computed from incomplete local history, and it does not block Save/Submit (`CLMViews.swift:1050-1070`). This is the single most consequential finding in this slice: if this hardcoded logic were ever mistaken for "the UCPMP rule is implemented on iPad," a rep could offline-drop far more than 12 packs and the client would show no error.

## 2. High-risk area #5 — UCPMP Annexure disclosure (SM-DIS-01..06)

Contrary to what the "does a disclosure PDF exist somewhere" framing in the audit task might suggest, this was **not a dead end** — a working, non-trivial disclosure generator already exists and reproduces the exact clue text. `UCPMPDisclosureController.cls` (Visualforce standard controller) and `pages/UCPMPDisclosureForm.page` (`renderAs="pdf"`) render the Annexure to clauses 5.1(g) and 14.4; `UCPMPComplianceService.generateDisclosureForm(fy)` (`cls:142-147`) calls `Page.UCPMPDisclosureForm.getContentAsPDF()`; and `UCPMPComplianceController.generateDisclosure(fy)` (`cls:65-70`) saves the result as a `ContentVersion` File and returns a download reference — a real, callable pipeline, not a mock. The methodology paragraph the controller auto-drafts (`UCPMPDisclosureController.cls:41-44`) is close to word-for-word what the spec's §17.2 demo fixture text describes, and it ends with **the exact sentence the audit task's clue is built around: "Source: Sciagen Sample Management ledger."** finding this is the headline result for this area. **Data sources:** company profile fields (CIN, name, address, email, PAN, signatory details) come from `UCPMP_Tenant_Config__c`, a Hierarchy Custom Setting (org-default singleton, currently unpopulated — 0 records in the sandbox, so every value renders blank today); the free-samples line comes from `Sample_Expenditure_Tracking__c` aggregated by `UCPMPComplianceService.getExpenditureSummary(fy)` (`cls:56-80`), summing `Total_Value_INR__c`/`Total_Packs_Given__c`/`Total_Units_Given__c` and counting `COUNT_DISTINCT(HCP_Account__c)` where packs given > 0 — which is precisely the spec's "distinct HCPs given ≥1 sample pack in the FY" definition. **Valuation formula:** matches clause 5.1(g) as coded (per-unit price to stockist or purchase price, packs × units-per-pack × per-unit value, snapshotted at time of supply) but, as noted in §1 above, without the FY-scoped average-price/provisional-vs-final machinery the clause and the spec both call for. **Recipient-HCP counting:** correct by construction (`COUNT_DISTINCT` on the same aggregation table). **PDF generation:** real and working (Visualforce PDF render + File save), but the education-programme lines are explicitly left blank for manual completion (`UCPMPDisclosureController.cls:1-5` doc comment: "Only the free samples line is produced by Sample Management; education programme lines are left for the company to complete") and the Digital Signature Certificate line renders empty — this is a **draft worksheet for a human to file externally**, not an automated regulatory submission, confirmed by the page's own footer text ("...Review before filing."). **Reproducibility of numbers:** the arithmetic is sound and would reproduce the demo fixture *if fed matching data* (₹250.0000 crore domestic sales → `UCPMP_Tenant_Config__c.Domestic_Sales_Revenue_INR__c = 2,500,000,000`; ₹0.0001 crore samples → a `Sample_Expenditure_Tracking__c` total of exactly ₹1,000 across 3 packs/12 units for 1 distinct HCP; company name "Allergopharma (demo)" → `Company_Name__c`) — but **none of that data exists today**: `UCPMP_Tenant_Config__c` has 0 saved records in the sandbox, `Sample_Expenditure_Tracking__c` has 0 rows, and no test class (`UCPMPComplianceServiceTest`, `UCPMPComplianceControllerTest`, `UCPMPDisclosureControllerTest`) exists anywhere in the 36 Apex test classes found (confirmed absent by name in `01_inventory_salesforce.md` §10.1). So: **the mechanism exists and is architecturally capable of the demo fixture; the demo fixture itself is neither seeded nor tested, and nothing today proves it actually reproduces those exact numbers.** What is genuinely missing, not just partial: there is no persisted, versioned, lockable `UCPMP_Disclosure__c`-equivalent record (every "generation" is a stateless re-render — SM-DIS-04 is a real gap, not just untested); no deadline/reminder/escalation model (SM-DIS-01); no association-membership or submission-workflow tracking (SM-DIS-02/03); and no configurable extension-date mechanism (SM-DIS-06). The one bright spot beyond the PDF itself is `UCPMPComplianceController.getHcpReport()`, which already gives a per-HCP drill-down table via the `ucpmpComplianceDashboard` LWC — a good foundation to extend into SM-DIS-05's number-level drill-down, rather than building it from nothing.

## 3. High-risk area #7 — Sharing & permissions (SM-SEC-01..04)

**Can reps see/edit other reps' lots or transactions?** The org-wide default on both `Sample_Lot__c` and `Sample_Transaction__c` is Private (confirmed via `<sharingModel>Private</sharingModel>` in each object's `.object-meta.xml`), and — unlike `Sciagen_Account__c`/`Planned_Call__c`/`RCPA_Audit__c`, which all have dedicated Apex-managed-sharing trigger/service pairs keeping their `__Share` tables in sync with territory assignment (`AccountTerritoryShareSync.trigger`, `PlannedCallTerritoryShareSync.trigger`, `RCPAAuditTerritoryShareSync.trigger`, `UserTerritoryShareSync.trigger`) — **no equivalent `__Share`-writing trigger exists for `Sample_Lot__c` or `Sample_Transaction__c` anywhere in the 22-trigger inventory.** That means, absent a role hierarchy grant, an ordinary rep's visibility into another rep's lots/transactions is governed only by owner + whatever role hierarchy exists — and the live role hierarchy itself is UNVERIFIED from source (no `roles/` directory is tracked in this repo). At the object-permission layer, `Sciagen_Mobile_App_Access` (the rep permission set) deliberately gives `Sample_Lot__c` **Read-only** and `Sample_Transaction__c` **Create/Read/Edit but not Delete** — a sensible ledger-not-lot-editing design — while `Sciagen_Sample_Admin` gets full CRUD + View All + Modify All on every sample object. Net effect: reps most likely **cannot** see or edit each other's lots/transactions today (private OWD, no widening sharing rule found), which is the right default, but this should be explicitly confirmed live (a retrieve of `Role` metadata) before the new module assumes it, since `Sample_Transaction__c`'s own service class is deliberately `without sharing` for *cross-owner writes during transfers* (`SampleTransactionService.cls:11`, "a transfer or receipt changes lots owned by another rep") — that is a controlled system-context bypass for the transfer mechanic, not a general visibility grant. **Is there an auditor read-only persona today?** No. No `Sample_Auditor` permission set exists anywhere in `permissionsets/` (11 sets total, none named for sample auditing), and there is no physical-inventory-count object in the schema at all for an auditor to reconcile against — SM-AUD-02 is a clean MISSING. A separate, materially important finding from the underlying inventory that bears directly on this area: `Consent__c` has Private OWD but **zero Apex-managed sharing**, and both broadly-assigned rep permission sets (`SciagenCrm`, `Sciagen_Mobile_App_Access`) grant `viewAllRecords=true`/`modifyAllRecords=true` on it — so the Private OWD on HCP consent/PII is effectively moot today; every rep can see and edit every HCP's consent record org-wide. That is not one of the 37 IDs in this slice directly, but it is a sharing-model precedent (and risk) worth carrying into the Sample Management build, since `Sample_Consent__c` in the full spec is architecturally adjacent.

## 4. Data migration needs (this slice's scope)

- None of the sample-inventory objects have any production data yet (`Sample_Lot__c`, `Sample_Transaction__c`, `Sample_Allocation__c`, `Sample_Limit__c` etc. are all 0 rows in the sandbox), so most changes in this slice are additive with no migration risk *today*.
- The one real exception is **SM-SEC-04 / SM-IOS-01 on iPad**: signature strokes and consent PII currently live inside a single UserDefaults JSON blob (`UserDataStore.swift`). Moving that data to an encrypted store or SwiftData (as the spec requires) needs an explicit migration path for any rep who already has unsynced local records — a silent format cutover would lose in-flight data.
- **SM-AUD-01**: turning on Field History Tracking (currently explicitly disabled on `Sample_Lot__c`, `Sample_Transaction__c`, `Call_Visit_Report__c`) does not retroactively create history for past changes, and introducing a hash-chained ledger needs a one-time bootstrap "genesis" entry before any further transactions are trusted — sequence this before real production sample volume, not after.

## 5. Breaking-change items (this slice's scope)

- **SM-AUD-01**: flipping `enableHistory` from `false` to `true` on `Sample_Lot__c`/`Sample_Transaction__c`/`Call_Visit_Report__c` is itself a metadata change with storage/limit implications or org preference toggles that should be confirmed live, not assumed safe by default.
- **SM-IOS-01/03/04**: replacing UserDefaults-blob persistence with SwiftData and adding a real rules engine both touch the app's core `UserDataStore`/`AccountStore` singletons that every other feature (CVR, RCPA, expenses, leave, KAM) currently depends on — this is an architecture-level change for the whole app, not an isolated sample-module addition, and needs careful staging to avoid regressing unrelated live features.
- **SM-DIS-04**: introducing a persisted, versioned `UCPMP_Disclosure__c` object changes the disclosure feature from "stateless PDF render on demand" to "stateful, lockable record" — any existing admin habit of just re-clicking "generate" for a fresh PDF would need retraining/UI change once locking exists.

## 6. Offline/online rule-parity gaps (this slice's scope)

This is the dominant theme of the whole slice, not an edge case: **every UCPMP rule with a working server implementation (SM-UC-05 12-pack limit, SM-UC-06 legend, SM-UC-09 hypnotic block, SM-UC-11 2% cap, SM-UC-12 valuation) has either no client-side mirror at all, or — in the single worst case (SM-UC-05) — an actively contradictory client-side mirror** (a non-UCPMP hardcoded cap of 48/36/60 packs vs. the legal 12, display-only, non-blocking). The spec's own working rule ("server is authoritative... the iPad can never create a compliant-looking record that the server would reject") is honored today only by accident, in the sense that there is no shared rules engine on iPad to be wrong in a rule-ID-mirroring sense — it is simply absent (SM-IOS-03), and the one ad hoc rule that does exist is wrong. SM-DIS (§17) is correctly N/A for iOS parity since it is an admin/web-only workflow.

## 7. Reusable strengths and worst gaps in this slice

**Top 5 surprisingly reusable pieces:**
1. `SampleLimitChecker`/`SampleLimitSeeder`/`SampleLimitGuardService` — the 12-pack UCPMP limit (SM-UC-05) is genuinely well-built: company-wide counting, tamper-resistant regulatory-limit guard rails, and a clean scope/period model that other limits (2%, per-call, territory) already reuse.
2. `UCPMPComplianceService`/`UCPMPDisclosureController`/`UCPMPDisclosureForm.page` — a real, working Annexure PDF generator whose methodology text and "Source: Sciagen Sample Management ledger" line already match the spec's demo fixture almost verbatim; a strong foundation to extend rather than replace for SM-DIS.
3. `Sample_Attempt__e` platform-event pattern (`SampleDropService.cls:67`, publishing a durable audit record for a refused sample line independent of the DML rollback) — a well-designed audit-trail technique worth reusing for SM-AUD-01's refusal evidence, even though the broader hash-chain/Field-History requirement is unmet.
4. The iPad app's generic OAuth/Keychain, CoreLocation, and camera/`UIImagePickerController` infrastructure (`SalesforceAuthService.swift`, `LocationService.swift`, `ImagePicker.swift`) — all real, working, and directly reusable for the signature-page GPS stamp (SM-IOS-04) and any new photo-based capture, once wired to sample-specific screens.
5. The iPad's generic delta-sync/idempotent-push/auto-sync-on-save engine (`SciagenAccountSyncService.cls`, `SalesforceRestDataService.swift`, `SyncCursorStore.swift`) — architecturally sound and a good template for SM-IOS-02, even though zero Sample_* objects are wired into it today.

**Top 5 worst gaps/conflicts in this slice:**
1. **SM-UC-05 on iOS** — a hardcoded, wrong, non-blocking yearly-limit UI that contradicts the actual UCPMP 12-pack rule; the single highest-risk item in this whole slice because it could look "done" at a glance.
2. **SM-IOS-01/03/04** — essentially the entire iPad sample-inventory data model, rules engine, and screen surface do not exist; this is XL, foundational, and blocks nearly everything else on device.
3. **SM-AUD-01** — Field History Tracking is explicitly turned off on the exact objects the spec says must have it, and there is no hash chain, no ledger-integrity job, and no `Sample_Ledger_Entry__c`-equivalent object at all.
4. **SM-DIS-04** — the disclosure "form" has no persisted, versioned, lockable backing record; every generation is a stateless, unlocked, unversioned PDF, which is a structural gap, not a UI gap.
5. **SM-UC-09 hypnotic/sedative/tranquillizer block** — works, but only via a generic, admin-discretionary "restricted" checkbox rather than a dedicated, self-enforcing therapeutic classification; a data-entry omission on one product would silently violate a "can never be sampled" legal requirement.
